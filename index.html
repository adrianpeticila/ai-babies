The 6 Pillars Glossary

Click any card to inspect the breakdown.

The Wires #01

Model Context Protocol (MCP)

What: An open standard to connect AI models to external tools and internal data.
Analogy: The universal USB-C port for artificial intelligence.
Why it matters: Connect your tools once and use them with any modern AI agent.
Takeaway: Standard protocols beat vendor lock-in every time.
The Engine #02

Tokens

What: The atomic pieces of words that language models read and write.
Analogy: Lego bricks or syllables of machine language.
Why it matters: Token count directly dictates your cloud bills and model speed.
Takeaway: Tokens are compute currency. Keep context trimmed.
The Engine #03

Context Window

What: The maximum number of tokens an AI can see at one time.
Analogy: The size of the physical desk where the AI is working.
Why it matters: When overloaded, earlier instructions drop off or degrade.
Takeaway: A bigger desk helps, but a clean desk prevents errors.
The Brain #04

Skills & Rules Hierarchy

What: Modular instruction folders loaded only when specifically needed.
Analogy: Standard Operating Procedure manuals stored on a bookshelf.
Why it matters: Keeps the main context tiny while enabling deep expertise on demand.
Takeaway: Load knowledge surgically, not all at once.
The Society #05

Orchestrator-Worker Loops

What: Dividing a complex task between specialized sub-agents.
Analogy: A general contractor managing an electrician and a plumber.
Why it matters: Prevents one giant context from getting tangled and confused.
Takeaway: Specialized small agents beat one monolithic prompt.
The Armor #06

Falsifiable Verifiers

What: Independent deterministic checks that must be able to fail.
Analogy: A smoke detector that you deliberately test with test smoke.
Why it matters: A verification step that cannot fail is just decorative theater.
Takeaway: The verifier must never be the producer.
