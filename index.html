Reference Architecture

The 6 Pillars Glossary

The Wires #01

Model Context Protocol (MCP)

What: An open standard to connect AI models to external tools and internal databases.
Analogy: The universal USB-C port for artificial intelligence.
Why it matters: Connect your tools once and use them with any modern AI agent.
Takeaway: Standard protocols beat vendor lock-in every time.
The Engine #02

Tokens

What: The atomic pieces of words that language models read and write.
Analogy: Lego bricks or syllables of machine language.
Why it matters: Token count directly dictates your cloud bills and model speed.
Takeaway: Tokens are compute currency. Keep context trimmed.
The Engine #03

Context Window

What: The maximum number of tokens an AI can process at one time.
Analogy: The physical surface area of the desk where the AI works.
Why it matters: When overloaded, earlier instructions drop off or degrade in precision.
Takeaway: A bigger desk helps, but a clean desk prevents hallucinations.
The Brain #04

Skills & Rules Hierarchy

What: Modular instruction folders loaded only when specifically invoked.
Analogy: Standard Operating Procedure binders kept neatly on a shelf.
Why it matters: Keeps the base prompt concise while unlocking deep specialized knowledge on demand.
Takeaway: Load knowledge surgically, never all at once.
The Society #05

Orchestrator-Worker Loops

What: Dividing a multi-step job between specialized sub-agents with bounded scope.
Analogy: A general contractor supervising a certified electrician and a plumber.
Why it matters: Eliminates single-thread context bloat and prevents hallucination spirals.
Takeaway: Specialized small agents beat monolithic mega-prompts.
The Armor #06

Falsifiable Verifiers

What: Independent deterministic checks that must be able to fail and catch errors.
Analogy: A smoke detector that you regularly trigger with test smoke.
Why it matters: A verification step that cannot fail is merely decorative theater.
Takeaway: The verifier must never be the producer.
