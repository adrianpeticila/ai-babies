# FAQ | AI for Babies

> Canonical: https://aiforbabies.pages.dev/faq/ | Last updated: 2026-10-14

## 1. Who is AI for Babies for?
Non-technical leaders who sit in AI meetings: CMOs, HR directors, founders, managers. People who need working understanding, not math.

## 2. Do I need a technical background?
No. Every concept is 150 words max plus one absurd analogy plus one job scenario. Zero jargon; jargon links to its own page.

## 3. What do I get for $9 vs $19?
Single Blueprint Pass ($9): one complete prompt/agent workflow. All-Access ($19): every blueprint, interactive tools (Prompt Engine, AGENTS.md Generator, Speed Lab, Agent Gym) plus updates.

## 4. Is my data collected?
No sensitive data collection. Static glossary plus newsletter; no account needed to read. Payment via Stripe/Gumroad only.

## 5. How is this different from an AI course?
No 40-hour course. One concept per page you can use in tomorrow's meeting. Newsletter: aah.monster-free, fluff-free.

## 6. Where do I read: site or Substack?
Both: site (aiforbabies.pages.dev) for glossary and tools, Substack (aiforbabies.substack.com) for the weekly dispatch.

## 7. Can I expense this?
Yes: $9/$19 one-time passes with receipts from Stripe/Gumroad. Most managers expense it as training under $25.

## 8. Do you cover agents, RAG, evals?
Yes: 77 terms plus RAG masterclass, AGENTS.md spec generator, 100k tool scaling notes, speculative decoding lab, WebMCP scanner, PRD framework.

## 9. Is there a team plan?
No team seat yet. All-Access is per-person; forward the expense receipt to your manager. Teams usually buy 3-5 singles.

## 10. How do I get help?
Substack reply or site contact. No calls, async answers. Start with the Dictionary, then Prompt Engine.
